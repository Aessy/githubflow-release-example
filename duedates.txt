Due Dates & Reminders
=====================
due_at: ISO 8601 datetime, optional, nullable
Filters: overdue=true, due_before, due_after

Reminders:
  POST/GET/DELETE  /tasks/:id/reminders[/:rid]
  Delivery: webhook (3x retry) → fallback to in-app GET /users/me/reminders

Recurring reminders:
  - "recurrence" accepts a cron expression (max every 1h)
  - Recurrence stops when task moves to done or cancelled
  - Maximum 5 active reminders per task per user

HOTFIX (v1.2.0.1):
  Recurring reminders were not cancelled when a task was marked done or
  cancelled — they continued firing indefinitely.
  Fix: status-change handler now explicitly cancels all active recurring
  reminders on the task. One-off reminders are unaffected.
