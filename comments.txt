Task Comments
=============
Comment model:
  - id, task_id, author_id, body (1-5000), created_at, updated_at, deleted_at

Endpoints:
  POST   /tasks/:id/comments
  GET    /tasks/:id/comments         Paginated, sorted oldest-first
  PATCH  /tasks/:id/comments/:cid    Editable within 15 min of posting
  DELETE /tasks/:id/comments/:cid    Soft-delete (author or admin)

Mentions:
  - @username syntax in body triggers a notification to the mentioned user
  - Mentions are resolved at write time; invalid usernames silently ignored
  - Maximum 10 mentions per comment

Markdown rendering:
  - Body stored as raw text; clients receive both raw and rendered_html fields
