Task Comments
=============
Comment model:
  - id, task_id, author_id, body (1-5000 chars), created_at, updated_at
  - deleted_at (soft-delete; body replaced with "[deleted]" on read)

Endpoints:
  POST   /tasks/:id/comments         Add comment (auth required, any user)
  GET    /tasks/:id/comments         List comments (paginated, auth required)
  PATCH  /tasks/:id/comments/:cid    Edit comment (author only, within 15 min)
  DELETE /tasks/:id/comments/:cid    Soft-delete comment (author or admin)
