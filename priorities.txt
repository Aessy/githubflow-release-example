Task Priorities
===============
Priority field: critical | high | normal | low  (default: normal)

Sorting:
  - sort=priority orders tasks critical→high→normal→low (desc) or reverse (asc)
  - Priority sort can be combined with secondary sort: ?sort=priority&sort=created_at

SLA targets (informational, not enforced by API):
  critical  — respond within 1h, resolve within 4h
  high      — respond within 4h, resolve within 24h
  normal    — respond within 24h
  low       — best effort
