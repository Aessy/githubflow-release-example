Webhooks
========
Webhook model: id, owner_id, url (https), events[], secret, created_at, active

CRUD: POST/GET/PATCH/DELETE /webhooks[/:id]
Events: task.created/updated/deleted, comment.created, reminder.triggered
Payload: { event, timestamp, data }  |  Signature: X-Taskr-Signature sha256 HMAC
Delivery: 5s timeout, 3x retry with backoff; auto-deactivate on exhaustion
Log: GET /webhooks/:id/deliveries  (last 50, 30-day retention)
