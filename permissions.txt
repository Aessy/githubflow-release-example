User Permissions and Teams
==========================
Team model: id, name, owner_id, members[{user_id, role}], created_at
Endpoints:  POST/GET/PATCH/DELETE /teams[/:id]
            POST/PATCH/DELETE /teams/:id/members[/:uid]

Task sharing:
  - POST /tasks accepts optional "team_id" to share with a team
  - Team tasks are visible to all team members (read)
  - editors and managers may update shared tasks
  - Only task owner or team manager may delete a shared task
  - A task may belong to at most one team
