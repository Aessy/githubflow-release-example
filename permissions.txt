User Permissions and Teams
==========================
Team model: id, name, owner_id, members[{user_id, role}]
Task sharing: optional team_id; visibility and edit rights governed by role

Permission matrix:
              | read | update | delete | change-priority | manage-members
  member      |  Y   |   -    |   -    |       -         |      -
  editor      |  Y   |   Y    |   -    |       Y         |      -
  manager     |  Y   |   Y    |   Y    |       Y         |      Y
  task owner  |  Y   |   Y    |   Y    |       Y         |      -
  admin       |  Y   |   Y    |   Y    |       Y         |      Y
